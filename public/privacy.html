PIXANIMALS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short

  • We do not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, sign-ups, ads, chat or trackers.
  • Your saved game stays only on your own device.
  • The game works offline and sends information to nobody.
  • This version has no real-money purchases.

Who is responsible

PIXANIMALS (“the game”) is published by NOMBRE_PENDIENTE. For any question about this policy, write to CORREO_PENDIENTE.

Who the game is for

PIXANIMALS is made for people aged 7 and over, including children. For that reason the game does not ask for or collect personal data from anyone, minors or adults.

Data we do NOT collect

The game does not ask for or collect: name, email address, age, date of birth, address, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or device identifiers (such as the advertising ID). It uses no analytics, statistics or advertising tools, and includes no third-party libraries that collect data.

The game has no chat, messages, leaderboards or any way to communicate with other people.

What is stored and where

  • Your saved game: your creatures, coins, items, story progress and settings (such as volume). It is stored on your device, in the game’s own storage (in the browser for the web version; in the app’s storage on Android), with backup copies on the same device so progress is not lost.
  • Creature nicknames: if you give a creature a nickname, that text is saved only inside your saved game. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Transfer code and file copy: in “Save” (Guardado) you can copy a code or download a file with your game to move it to another device. They are created on your device and handled by you.

On Android, the system may include the app’s data in the Google account backup of the device, if that backup is turned on in the phone. That backup is managed by Google in the account of the person using the phone, under Google’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to it.

Internet connection

The game works completely offline and does not send data to any server of ours or of third parties.

Web version: the game page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, the server that delivers it (GitHub) may log technical data about the visit, such as the IP address, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The browser also stores the game files so it can open offline.

Purchases and random content

  • This version has no real-money purchases and no advertising.
  • Creature packs are obtained by playing, with coins earned inside the game, and always show their odds.
  • If optional purchases are added in the future, they will be processed by Google Play (we will not see payment details), will ask for an adult’s confirmation, will have a daily limit and will show the odds before buying. This policy will be updated before they exist.

How to delete your data

Since we do not keep your data on any server, there is nothing you need to ask us to delete: everything is on your device and under your control. You can erase your game from Save (Guardado) → Delete game (Borrar partida), by uninstalling the app, or by clearing the site data in the browser.

Parents and guardians can do this on the child’s device, or ask us any question at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes (for example, if purchases are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the game.
